Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who we are

AquaHub Connect is operated by EcoCharge Ghana Limited ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices, in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address and phone number.
  • Shop, driver and collector details: shop name, category, area, vehicle type and plate number, and the price you set.
  • Payout details: the mobile money or bank account number and provider you give us, so we can pay you. We never see your mobile money PIN or card details; payments are handled by Paystack.
  • Location: only when you choose to share it: your GPS pin and Ghana Post code for a request, a driver's live position during an accepted job, and your area for local weather.
  • Photos: product pictures you upload, your profile picture and business logo (visible to other users), and pickup photos taken by collectors.
  • Activity: orders, bookings, waste collection subscriptions and payments, ratings, and notifications.
  • Alert settings: your phone's push notification address, and your phone number if you turn on SMS alerts.

How we use it

To run AquaHub: connect customers with shops, drivers and collectors, take payments and pay out money, send reminders and receipts, show alerts and weather, keep the platform safe, and improve the service. We do not sell your data.

Who sees it

  • Other users, only as needed: a collector sees the name, address and phone of households that join them; a household sees the collector's name and pickup photos for its own pickups; a driver sees your phone and location after a job is accepted; a seller sees your name, phone and delivery address for a paid order. Shop names, areas and ratings are public.
  • Service providers that help us run AquaHub: Supabase (database and storage), Vercel (hosting), Paystack (payments and payouts), Arkesel (SMS, only if you opt in), Open-Meteo (weather), and your phone's push service (Apple, Google or your browser's provider). Some of these process data outside Ghana.
  • The law: we may share information when the law or a court requires it, or to prevent fraud.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. Live driver locations are deleted when a job ends. When you delete your account we delete your profile, requests, orders, ratings and notifications. Payment records may be kept as long as the law requires, and Paystack keeps its own records.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Delete your account in the app at Profile, then Delete Account. You can turn off push and SMS alerts in the app, and stop sharing location in your phone settings. You may also complain to Ghana's Data Protection Commission.

Children

AquaHub is not directed at children under 13, and shops, drivers and collectors must be 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from young children. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use access rules and encrypted connections, but no system is completely secure. Tell us at once if you think your account was misused.

Changes

We will post changes on this page with a new date.
