Who we are

AquaHub Connect is operated by EcoCharge Ghana Limited ("we", "us"), [address]. Contact: [email], [phone]. This policy explains what we collect and why, in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

What we collect

  • Account details: name, phone number and email address.
  • Location: only when you choose to share it, to give a driver your service location, to show a driver's live position during an accepted job, or to show local weather. Drivers share their location only after tapping Start trip, and it is deleted when the job ends.
  • Activity: requests, quotes, orders, ratings, product listings, notifications and game progress.
  • Payments: handled by Paystack. We do not see or store your card or mobile money details.
  • On your device: settings such as your chosen town and language.

How we use it

To run the service: connect customers with drivers and sellers, process deposits and orders, show alerts and weather, prevent abuse, and improve AquaHub.

Who sees it

  • A driver sees your phone number and location only after the job is confirmed. A seller sees your name, phone and delivery address only for a paid order.
  • Service providers that help us run AquaHub: Supabase (hosting and database), Paystack (payments) and Open-Meteo (weather, using approximate coordinates, not your identity).
  • We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep account data until you delete your account. Live driver locations are deleted when a job ends. When you delete your account we delete your profile, requests, orders, ratings and notifications. Payment records held by Paystack follow their own rules and the law.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. You can delete your account in the app at Profile, then Delete Account, or by contacting us at [email]. You may also complain to Ghana's Data Protection Commission.
