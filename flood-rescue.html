FLOODRESCUE

Plan. Rescue. Save lives.

How to Play

Goal: rescue at least 10 residents, take them to the green Evacuation Centre, and visit the white Clinic before time runs out.

Drive: W A S D or arrow keys, or the on-screen joystick.
Rescue / Drop off: Space, or the big orange button, when it lights up near a resident or a centre.
Watch out: the water keeps rising. Hitting houses and cars damages the boat. The engine burns fuel, and the Evacuation Centre refuels you. The boat holds 4 people.
Pause: Esc or P, or the Pause button.
