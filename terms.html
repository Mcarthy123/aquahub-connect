It is a platform where households and customers buy from shops, book drivers (such as toilet and pit draining, water delivery and garbage collection) and pay waste collectors. Shops, drivers and collectors are independent businesses. They are not our employees or agents, they set their own prices, and the sale or service is between you and them. By using AquaHub you accept these terms.

2. Your account

Use accurate details and keep your login private. You are responsible for what happens on your account. You must be 18 or older to register as a seller, driver or collector. We may suspend or close accounts that break these terms or put others at risk. You can delete your account in the app at Profile, then Delete Account.

3. Yearly registration fee

Sellers, drivers and collectors pay a registration fee through Paystack before their account can be approved, and renew it every year: GH₵50 a year for sellers, GH₵550 a year for waste collectors, and GH₵200 a year for vacuum trucks and water tankers. The fee is shown before you pay and you must agree to it. We remind you before your year ends. If you do not renew, your account is paused: it is no longer listed and cannot take new orders, bookings or customers until you renew, and your history is kept. Waste collection customers you already have can still pay you. If we reject your application we refund the fee. Once your account is approved, a fee you have paid is not refundable unless the law requires it. We may change fees with notice in the app; the amount shown when you pay is the amount you pay.

4. Commission and payouts

AquaHub keeps a commission of 8% of each payment you receive through the app (for example shop orders and waste collection fees). The other 92% is paid to the bank or mobile money account you give us. Payment charges from Paystack are covered by AquaHub out of its commission. We may change the commission with notice in the app; it will not apply to payments already made.

Payouts are made by Paystack, usually the next working day. The first payout to a new or changed account can be delayed while Paystack verifies it, and Paystack may hold small bank payouts until they reach its minimum. You are responsible for entering correct payout details. We are not responsible for money sent to details you entered wrongly.

5. Shop orders

Sellers must list goods honestly, deliver what was ordered and handle returns fairly. Customers pay through the app and should check goods on delivery. Do not sell illegal, counterfeit, stolen or dangerous items.

6. Driver bookings

A customer requests a verified driver, and the driver accepts or declines. Drivers set their own prices and must be licensed, insured where required, and provide safe and legal service. Ratings must be honest.

7. Waste collection subscriptions

A household joins a collector and pays the collector's monthly fee in the app, on any day of the month. We send reminders by notification and, if you opt in, by SMS. A payment is overdue after a short grace period at the start of the next month. You receive a receipt for each payment. Collectors set pickup days and can log pickups with a photo; households can report a missed pickup. Anyone can also report an overflowing community bin to a collection company through the app. The company decides when to collect it. Disputes about the service are between the household and the collector, and we may help them settle it.
